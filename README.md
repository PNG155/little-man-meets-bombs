# little-man-meets-bombs
You are a tiny man who, through a twist of fate, ends up in a multiversal fluctuation connected by interdimensional threads. The chaos—triggered by the atomic bombs that fell on your home—is causing quite a problem; fortunately, being so small, you can dodge the blasts and slip between the ionically charged atoms surrounding you. Play carefully, or you might end up in limbo



Hey, I made this game for a game jam. We were supposed to have 3 days (72 hours) to build it, but I ended up going away on a trip for 2 days and also had to study for an exam.

I wound up creating the ENTIRE game—the concept, music, sprites, and all the programming—in just 3 hours.

It’s not one of my best pieces of work; honestly, I’m not really proud of it, but hey—at least I managed to put together something that’s at least minimally functional
